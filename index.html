About the Council

Where Ideas Meet Industry

The Industry & Innovation Council (IIC) at SRMIST Ramapuram is established under the Ministry of Education's Innovation Cell (MIC) to systematically foster the culture of innovation, startups, and entrepreneurship among students and faculty.

The council serves as the central hub for coordinating all innovation and industry-related activities across the campus â€” nurturing creativity and transforming ideas into impactful, real-world solutions.

Vision & Mission

Driving innovation and industry-readiness across the campus

Our Vision

To build a thriving innovation ecosystem at SRMIST Ramapuram that empowers students and faculty to become industry-ready innovators, entrepreneurs, and leaders who create real-world impact.

Our Mission

  • Industry-Academia Linkages: Establishing strong corporate, R&D, and research partnerships.
  • Mentorship & Guidance: Providing expert technical, business, and IP guidance.
  • Startup Support: Supporting and incubating student and faculty ventures.
  • Hands-on Learning: Enabling experiential learning through research, live projects, and hackathons.
